Rules
  1. Place a stone from your reserve on an empty point.
  2. Capture, if you wish, the first stone met from the placed stone along one of its two diagonals, if it belongs to your opponent and you already had more stones than your opponent on that diagonal before placing. Your own stones block the line; one capture per turn at most; captured stones leave the game for good.
  3. End when both reserves are empty. Each of the 18 routes (9 rows, 9 columns) goes to the side with more stones on it; diagonals do not score. Most routes wins; on a tie, most stones on the board.
Off the board, at the end of each route: a dot in the colour of the side holding it, and the White · Black stone count.
