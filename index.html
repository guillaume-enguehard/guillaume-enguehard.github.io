DORON

Move the red doron in a straight line, then drop a stone on the square it leaves. You never choose where your own stone lands: your opponent does. Build the largest group.

Players
2
Length
15 min
Ages
8+

TOLL

Place one stone per turn. Each row and column goes to whoever holds the majority on it, but captures run along the diagonals, and only if you were already the stronger side there. Hold the most of the 18 routes.

Players
2
Length
20–30 min
Ages
10+
Play online Rules (PDF) Manual (PDF, fr)

NORMAN TAFL

A symmetric tafl: both kings start side by side on the central throne. Split the pair, then lead your king to a corner or catch the enemy king between two of your pawns. Every threat must be answered, and you win when no answer is left.

Players
2
Length
30–45 min
